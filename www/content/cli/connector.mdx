```bash
ocel connector add
```

`add` puts a connector on the target this project's config names and tells the console where to
dial it. Sign in and link the directory first, with [`ocel login`](/docs/cli/login) and
[`ocel link`](/docs/link).

A connector lets the console do everything a person's role allows on this target: read the
deployed apps and act on them (a rollback, a hostname, protection), and read, write and reveal
variables. What each person may do is still capped by their role in the console.

`--compute` picks what the connector runs on, `serverless` or `container`; otherwise the provider's
default.

## One connector per target

A second `add` against the same target repoints the connector already there. That is also how you
finish an add that stopped partway.
