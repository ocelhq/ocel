---
title: provider.denied
description: The provider refused what the command asked, as not permitted.
---

The provider answered that what the command asked is not permitted: the account's credentials may not do it, or a limit, such as how many previews a plan runs at once, refuses it. The message is the provider's own and names what it refused; a provider that names its own code puts it first, as in `cloud.preview_limit: …`.

## Fix

Do what the message names: grant the credentials what they lack, remove what holds the limit, or raise it.
