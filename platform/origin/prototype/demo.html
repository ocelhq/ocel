Roles and Backings

An app declares what it needs — a bucket, a Postgres database. The config names one origin that runs the app's code, and may name a backing for any other role: the edge, DNS, or a kind of resource. Everything unnamed falls to the origin's default. Before anything is provisioned, the plan checks that every role the app needs is filled by something that can actually reach the app.

The question this answers: if providers are not all-or-nothing, does "one origin + per-role backings, resolved at plan time" keep the promise that app code never changes — and does it refuse the right things, with the right fix, before touching the cloud?
